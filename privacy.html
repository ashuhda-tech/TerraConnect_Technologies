Title: TerraConnect Data Processing & AI Consent Agreement 1. Data Collection By creating an account on TerraConnect, you consent to the collection and storage of your registered data. This includes your account details, environmental telemetry from your IoT sensors (temperature, humidity, CO2, etc.), animal biological data (weight, feeding, shedding), veterinary records, and geolocation data. 2. Third-Party Artificial Intelligence Processing To provide you with the "AI Insights" feature, you explicitly consent to TerraConnect transmitting your environmental and biological data to third-party Artificial Intelligence providers (specifically, Google Gemini API). Free Tier Notice: While in our development/free tiers, the AI provider may temporarily log prompts and generated responses to improve their machine-learning models. Please do not upload highly sensitive human personal information (such as personal medical records or government IDs) to the platform. 3. Data Storage & Security Your data is securely routed and stored using Google Cloud infrastructure (Google Sheets / Google Apps Script). We do not sell your personal data or animal telemetry to advertisers or data brokers. 4. Right to Revoke & Delete You maintain full ownership of your data. You have the right to revoke this consent at any time by deleting your account. Upon account deletion, your historical telemetry, biological records, and configurations will be permanently erased from our active databases.
