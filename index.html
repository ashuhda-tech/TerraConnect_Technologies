Cultivating Synergy

Technology and nature operating in perfect balance

The Vision

To cultivate a highly conscious world where technology and nature operate in perfect synergy, guided by a profound respect for the whole.

We envision a future of decentralized self-governance, where society is so socially and ecologically conscious and responsible that collective harmony—and harmony with the surrounding environment—becomes a natural byproduct of our collective consciousness. In this future, the barriers between humans, animals, and the environment dissolve into a cohesive, harmonious ecosystem. HARPIA WorldConnect exists to reflect this utopian potential through technological development; we believe that when human beings are equipped with educational information, transparency, and high-quality solutions, they naturally choose collective empathy, respect, and balance with their surroundings.

The Mission

To democratize high-quality environmental data management tools and automation, bridging the knowledge gap between human awareness and natural habitats in order to foster a profound balance and collective respect among living beings and their environments.

As a non-profit initiative, our goal is not to extract value from users or nature; rather, operating at cost and leveraging technology and communities, we aim to generate synergy among them. Step by step—micro-ecosystem by micro-ecosystem—we provide the open-source tools and collective knowledge needed to deepen our understanding of the environments with which we interact. We are building a freely shared database—while respecting data protection laws—to empower individuals to become conscious stewards of their natural world.

Our Pillars

Radical Transparency

We operate with a commitment to legal responsibility, aiming for maximum transparency regarding knowledge, usability, and open-source principles. High trust is the core systemic value of our operations; it is freely fostered through unrestricted access to our shared knowledge, projects, and intentions, with the goal of building a community based on deep mutual trust.

Decentralized Stewardship

We believe in the power of the informed individual to create collective value. By making the systemic flow of information and the process of ensuring its quality transparent, we eliminate the need for centralized control, empowering a self-governing, value-adding community of responsible caregivers.

Synergy Over Profit

We are driven by the cohesive functioning of society and nature. Every piece of hardware built and every line of code written is evaluated by its capacity to improve the social fabric, rather than by its potential to generate capital.

Reverence for the Whole

We recognize that the observer, the tool, and the habitat form an interconnected system. Our technology aims to serve as a bridge for connection, respecting and valuing all three simultaneously.

Indigenous Organizations & Support

COIAB

Coordination of Indigenous Organizations of the Brazilian Amazon: Founded in 1989, it is Brazil's largest regional indigenous organization, coordinating the political and territorial defense of over a hundred peoples in the region.

APIB

Articulation of Indigenous Peoples of Brazil: The main representative body for the indigenous movement in the country, unifying agendas regarding land demarcation and rights within the National Congress and the Supreme Federal Court (STF).

CGY

Guarani Yvyrupa Commission: Brings together Guarani collectives from the South and Southeast regions, working actively on the regularization and protection of traditional lands in the Atlantic Forest.

ISA

Socio-Environmental Institute (Instituto Socioambiental): One of the largest NGOs in the sector. It conducts research, satellite monitoring to combat deforestation, and provides direct legal counsel to traditional peoples.

CIMI

Indigenous Missionary Council: Operates across all Brazilian regions, providing annual reports on violence against indigenous peoples and offering support during legal land demarcation processes.

CTI

Center for Indigenous Work (Centro de Trabalho Indigenista): Develops environmental management and economic sustainability projects with ethnic groups such as the Guarani, Timbira, and the peoples of the Javari Valley.

Kanindé

Ethno-environmental Defense Association: Nationally renowned for developing monitoring systems for indigenous lands threatened by invasions and for preserving local flora and fauna.

Environmental & Wildlife NGOs

WWF-Brasil

Dozens of simultaneous projects focused on biodiversity conservation and the sustainable use of natural resources.

SOS Atlantic Forest Foundation

Has facilitated the planting of over 44 million trees and coordinates river monitoring efforts involving thousands of volunteers.

IPÊ

Institute for Ecological Research: Founded with a focus on protecting the black lion tamarin, it expanded its scope to major projects in the Amazon, Pantanal, and Atlantic Forest.

Greenpeace Brasil

Focused on peaceful activism and social mobilization against deforestation and in support of renewable energy.

TNC Brasil

The Nature Conservancy: Implements science-based strategies to protect vast areas of forest and watersheds.

RENCTAS

A national leader in generating data and reports to combat the illegal trade and trafficking of Brazilian wildlife.

Instituto Vida Livre

Works directly on the rescue, rehabilitation, and release of wild animals that have suffered accidents or were seized from traffickers in Rio de Janeiro.

Santuário de Elefantes Brasil (SEB)

Dedicated to rescuing elephants formerly held in captivity in Latin America and providing them with spacious, natural habitats.

Instituto Arara Azul

Instrumental in removing the hyacinth macaw from the list of endangered species through the monitoring of nests and populations in the Pantanal.

AMPARA Silvestre

Focused on mitigating human impact by supporting conservation projects for big cats, maned wolves, and native wildlife.

SOS Fauna

Operates in enforcement, intelligence gathering, and the direct rescue of wild animals that are victims of interstate trafficking networks.

Projeto Tamar

One of the country's longest-running initiatives, protecting sea turtles across more than 20 coastal locations.
